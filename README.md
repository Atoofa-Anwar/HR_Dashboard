# HR_Dashboard
Objective

The HR department wants to create an HR Analytics dashboard to analyze employee attrition.
The objective is to understand employee demographics, attrition trends, and key factors influencing employee turnover, so that the organization can improve retention strategies.

Sample Questions

What is the total number of employees in the organization?

How many employees have left the company (attrition)?

How many employees are currently active?

What is the overall attrition rate?

What is the average age of employees?

What is the gender-wise distribution of employees?

Which education level has the highest attrition?

Which job roles have the highest attrition?

Which department is most affected by attrition?

Which age group shows the highest attrition?

How does marital status impact employee attrition?

Sample Insights

The total number of employees is 1470, out of which 237 employees have left the organization

The overall attrition rate is 16.12%

The average age of employees is 37 years

Male employees (60%) are more compared to female employees (40%)

Employees with Bachelor’s Degree show the highest attrition

Laboratory Technician and Sales Executive roles have the highest attrition

The R&D department contributes the most to total attrition

Employees in the 25–34 age group have the highest attrition

Married employees show higher attrition compared to single and divorced employees
